## Sounds

All four samples (card flick, paper slide, needle thump, crackle loop) are synthesised in
`src/audio/sounds.ts` from filtered noise and sine partials. They are original to this project, so no licence
applies. If you replace them with recordings in `public/sounds/*.ogg`, each file must be CC0 or self-recorded
and under 60 KB; list them here with source and licence:

## Textures and artwork

- Room surfaces (walnut, floorboards, plaster, rug, night window, prints, speaker cloth, leaves) are drawn
  procedurally in `src/scene/textures.ts`; room lighting is baked at startup in `src/scene/bake.ts`.
- Sleeve backs and vinyl labels are generated from album metadata in `src/scene/sleeveArt.ts`.
- Mock-library covers are generated by `ingest/mock.py`. They use whatever system fonts are installed on the
  machine that runs the generator (DejaVu, Liberation or FreeSans on Linux) and are never committed.

## Data

- Album metadata and cover art come from the Spotify Web API for the owner's personal, private,
  non-commercial use, cached locally by the ingest script. Keep `data/` out of any public repository.
- Optional enrichment uses the MusicBrainz API (core data CC0; tags and genres CC BY-NC-SA 3.0) and the Cover
  Art Archive, at MusicBrainz's requested rate of one request per second.
