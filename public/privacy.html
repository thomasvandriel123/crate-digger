Privacy

Crate Digger is a static website. It has no accounts, no database, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. Your music library stays in your own browser.

If you upload your Spotify data export

  • The file is read in your browser. It is never sent to this site's server or anyone else. Only the list of saved albums (artist, title and Spotify link) is kept, in your browser's local storage.
  • To find covers, release years and genres, your browser asks MusicBrainz about each album (artist and title only) and loads cover images from the Cover Art Archive, which is hosted by the Internet Archive. These services see your IP address, as with any website you visit.
  • "Forget my records" in the corner menu deletes all of it from your browser.

If you connect Spotify

  • You log in on Spotify's own page. This site asks for read access to your saved albums, and for permission to play music in the browser tab (Spotify Premium). It never sees your password.
  • Your browser talks to Spotify directly. The access token and a copy of your saved albums are kept in your browser's local storage, so the room opens quickly next time. Nothing is stored on this site's server.
  • "Disconnect Spotify" deletes the token and the copy. You can also remove the app's access at any time on spotify.com/account/apps.

The server

The web server sends the site's files. It does not keep access logs, and it receives nothing about your library.

Attribution

Album data and artwork are from Spotify when you connect your account. For uploaded libraries, metadata is from MusicBrainz (CC0) and cover images are from the Cover Art Archive; the images belong to their respective rights holders. Crate Digger is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.
